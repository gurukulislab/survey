How it works

The plumbing produces the intelligence. The intelligence produces your short list.

For the principal who wants the mechanism rather than the pitch: three layers, four visible rules, one loop, and a plain account of what we choose not to do.

Three layers

One record. An intelligence layer. A short list for today.

1

One record

Admissions, fees, transport, attendance, learning and parents all write to the same record. It is entered once, by the person already doing the work, so nobody keeps a second copy.

What we deliberately don't do

Three choices we made on purpose.

We report at class and school level.Figures on the management dashboard are at class and school level. Feedback is anonymous and reported as groups and trends.
We show counts, not lists of families.The transport corridor view shows counts of households, never a list of families to market to.
We never hide the rule.Early warnings show the rule that fired, so a teacher can disagree with it.
